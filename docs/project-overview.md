# Bunkd Project Overview

## Problem

Students often face long queues, limited visibility into available food options, and fragmented commerce experiences inside campuses.

## Solution

Bunkd is a campus commerce platform that enables students to discover food stalls, place pickup orders, explore hostel marketplace listings, and interact with a unified campus ecosystem.

## Core Modules

### Campus Mode
Browse stalls, menus, categories, and place pickup orders.

### Hostel Mode
Buy snacks and essentials from hostel sellers.

### Operations Hub
Monitor platform activity, vendor health, and operational performance.

### Audience Intelligence
Analyze student engagement, behavior patterns, search trends, and growth metrics.
